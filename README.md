# Product Adoption & Customer Retention

> Analysed ABC Pay Flow’s customer adoption and usage behaviour to identify product engagement barriers and develop evidence-based strategies to improve meaningful and sustained usage.

## About the Project

ABC Pay is a fictional Luxembourg-based FinTech startup providing digital payment and financial management solutions to small and medium-sized businesses across Europe.

This project investigates the performance of **ABC Pay Flow**, a new invoice and payment automation product launched on 1 July 2024, by analysing customer, platform activity, product activity, and subscription data covering **January 2023 to December 2025**.

The analysis focuses on whether customers progressed beyond initial product activation into **meaningful adoption and sustained usage**, identifying where customers dropped off and which behaviours and customer characteristics were associated with stronger adoption and retention.

## Objective

The objective was to understand:

- How ABC Pay Flow was performing across the customer adoption journey.
- Where customers were dropping off between eligibility, activation, and meaningful adoption.
- Which customer behaviours and segments were associated with stronger or weaker adoption and retention.
- Whether meaningful adoption translated into sustained product usage over time.
- How the findings could inform strategies to improve customer onboarding, adoption, and ongoing engagement.

## Project Approach

The investigation followed a progressive product analytics approach, moving from the size of the Flow opportunity towards adoption, behavioural diagnosis, segmentation, and sustained usage.

**Flow Opportunity → Adoption Funnel → Meaningful Usage → Segment Performance → Adoption vs Retention → Behavioural Diagnosis → Strategic Recommendations**

This allowed the analysis to move from measuring initial product performance to identifying adoption barriers, understanding customer behaviour, and developing evidence-based recommendations for improving long-term product engagement.

## Key Result

The analysis found that **42.2% of Flow-eligible customers activated the product**, but only **16.3% of adoption-eligible customers became adopters** by completing at least three meaningful Flow actions within 30 days.

The analysis identified the largest opportunity in the **transition from activation to meaningful adoption**, with **2,447 activated customers (83.7%)** failing to progress to the adoption threshold after completing the required observation period.

Early meaningful usage was also strongly associated with eventual adoption: **45.0% of adopters reached their first meaningful usage within 14 days**, compared with only **4.6% of activated non-adopters**.

Retention analysis further showed that **90-day retention stood at 61.3%**, demonstrating that adoption alone did not guarantee sustained engagement.

The project translated these findings into four strategic priorities: **strengthening the activation-to-adoption journey, establishing early-engagement interventions, introducing segment-specific adoption strategies, and building a sustained usage programme**.
